What the results mean

Results are based on available ingredient data and should not be treated as certification.

  • Likely Jain-friendly

    No obvious meat, egg, onion, garlic, or root-vegetable concern found in available data.

  • Contains Jain-restricted vegetables

    Contains onion, garlic, root vegetables, or related ingredients that many Jains avoid.

  • Needs verification

    Contains egg, unclear ingredients, or ingredients that may be animal-derived or ambiguous.

  • Not Jain-friendly

    Contains meat, fish, gelatin from animal sources, or other clearly non-Jain ingredients.

  • Not enough data

    Product was not found or ingredient data is incomplete. Read the label directly.

How JainScan decides

  • Checks available ingredient text and product metadata from multiple food databases
  • Flags ingredients commonly avoided in Jain diets (meat, fish, eggs, onion, garlic, root vegetables)
  • Marks unclear or ambiguous ingredients for manual verification
  • Cannot guarantee manufacturer accuracy — formulations can change at any time
  • Provides a confidence level based on completeness of available ingredient data

Confidence levels

High
Clear ingredient list with direct matches to known Jain categories
Medium
Partial ingredient data or some normalized ingredient mapping
Low
Sparse or ambiguous ingredient text — always verify label directly
