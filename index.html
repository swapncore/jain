        What the results mean

        Results are based on available ingredient data and should not be treated as certification.

        • Jain-Friendly

          No obvious meat, egg, onion, garlic, or root-vegetable concern found in available data.

        • Jain-Restricted

          Contains ingredients restricted in your current mode — root vegetables, onion/garlic, or in stricter modes: honey, fermented ingredients, and leafy greens.

        • Uncertain / Ambiguous

          Contains ingredients like mono- and diglycerides, natural flavors, or enzymes whose source (plant vs. animal) is not declared. Verify with the manufacturer if unsure.

        • Meat Detected

          Contains meat, fish, gelatin, carmine (E120), shellac (E904), or other clearly non-Jain animal-derived ingredients.

        • Not enough data

          Product was not found or ingredient data is incomplete. Read the label directly.

        How Jaini decides
        • Checks available ingredient text and product metadata from multiple food databases
        • Flags ingredients commonly avoided in Jain diets (meat, fish, eggs, onion, garlic, root vegetables)
        • Marks unclear or ambiguous ingredients for manual verification
        • Cannot guarantee manufacturer accuracy. Formulations can change at any time.
        • The verdict depends on your selected mode — tap the mode pills above to switch between Everyday Jain, Temple, Paryushan, and Greens+ standards. Learn what each mode means →
